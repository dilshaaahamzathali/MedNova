How MedNova Works

Confidential Healthcare in 3 Simple Steps

We decoupled personal identities from clinical discussions so you can seek medical guidance without anxiety or stigma.

01

Join With A Pseudonym

Sign up using your chosen alias or let our Zero-Knowledge generator assign you a shielded pseudonym (e.g., AnonPatient#8F2A9C). Your real identity is never exposed publicly.

02

Seek Peer or Doctor Care

Share real experiences in specialized disease communities (Oncology, HIV, Cardiology, Diabetes) or request 1-on-1 consultations with verified practitioners.

03

AES-256 Protected Resolution

All symptoms and journals are encrypted at rest with AES-256. Only your assigned certified doctor decrypts your case to provide prescriptions and clinical guidance.

Platform Architecture

Engineered For Absolute Medical Confidentiality
